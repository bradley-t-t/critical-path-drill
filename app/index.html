Critical Path Drill

Type your answers straight into the boxes. Each one turns green or red when you leave it, and red ones tell you the rule to fix it.

Method:
Order to work in ESDurEFActivityLSFloatLF
  1. Forward pass left to right: fill ES and EF in the top row.
  2. Backward pass right to left: fill LF and LS in the bottom row.
  3. Float = LS − ES. Zero float means critical.
  4. Critical path: click the activity names on it, then press Check critical path.
Click a box to start. Tip: begin with the ES of the first activity.
